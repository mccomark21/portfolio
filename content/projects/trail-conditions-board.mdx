The official park pages say a trail is open or closed. They do not say that the north loop is ankle-deep mud after yesterday's rain. That kind of news travels through group chats and gets lost.

The app shows a map of about forty trailheads. Tapping one shows the latest reports, each with a condition, an optional photo, and the time it was posted. Reports fade after three days and disappear after seven. Anyone can read the board. Posting needs a magic-link sign-in so that spam stays manageable.

Supabase handles auth, storage for photos, and a Postgres table of reports with row-level security, so a user can only edit their own posts. The map uses Leaflet with OpenStreetMap tiles.

The biggest open problem is trust. Two reports for the same trail on the same afternoon sometimes disagree. I am experimenting with showing the agreement between recent reports instead of only the newest one.
